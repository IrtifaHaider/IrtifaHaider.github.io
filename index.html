About

I am an MSc student working on Natural Language Processing, with a focus on Bangla, a language that still has far fewer ready-made NLP resources than English. My recent work studies how text optimization and transformer-based language models can improve Bangla text summarization. I am also working on the reliability of large language models in low-resource languages, and I am interested in information retrieval and graph-based data mining.

The Star Paradigm: Redefining Social Network Clustering

Manuscript in preparation B.Sc. thesis, supervised by Dr. Sanjit Kumar Saha

4baseline methods
3evaluation phases
250–2,000nodes in scalability tests
0–30%noise edges tested
  • Proposed and evaluated a graph-based star clustering approach for social network analysis.
  • Compared the ISOCD variants against Original Star clustering, Louvain, Label Propagation and Greedy Modularity on classic networks and LFR benchmark graphs, using modularity, coverage, NMI, runtime and community count.
  • Tested the incremental setting by streaming nodes into LFR graphs from a 40% seed set, and studied sensitivity to the parameter τ.
  • Evaluated scalability from 250 to 2,000 nodes, the effect of node arrival order (random, preferential, BFS) and robustness to noise edges.
